The subscription

  • Nefertiti is a free download. Opening the app needs a subscription: monthly or yearly. The price shown on the plan screen, in your currency, is the price you pay.
  • Every new subscriber gets 1 month free. The free month is offered once for each Apple ID.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID when the free month ends, and then at the start of each period.
  • A plan renews on its own unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Your Apple ID is charged for the renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Manage or cancel any time in Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel the plan. If you cancel during the free month, you are not charged.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • If a plan ends, your studies stay on the iPad, encrypted. They open again when you subscribe.
  • Restore purchases on the plan screen brings back a plan you already bought with the same Apple ID.

Your data

Nefertiti collects no data. Apple handles the purchase and the payment. We never see your name, card or Apple ID. Read the privacy policy.
